Amit Karmon

Postdoctoral Fellow at the Edelstein Center, Hebrew University of Jerusalem,
and DPhil candidate at the University of Oxford.

I work at the intersection of epistemology, philosophy of science, and theoretical computer science. My research uses ideas from computational learning theory, computational complexity theory, and algorithmic information theory to study induction, confirmation, and the epistemology of computationally bounded agents.
